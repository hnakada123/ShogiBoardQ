Piece Styles

21 piece styles with clear Mincho lettering

Piece Selection Menu

Open View → Piece Style to choose Standard Pieces or one of 20 variations. The new styles are grouped into Tiger Grain, Wood Tones, Light Colors, and Dark Colors. Your selection applies immediately and is restored on the next launch.

The former wooden set is now the standard, with its artwork unchanged. Saved selections of retired sets automatically migrate to Standard Pieces.

The same pieces appear on the main board, in hand, in principal variations, and in tsume boards. Adjust colors, piece size, and shadows in Board Colors.

Standard Pieces

Pale wood, fine outlines, subtle grain, and Mincho lettering

Promoted pieces use red lettering. Promoted lance, knight, and silver are marked 杏, 圭, and 全.

20 Variations

Every variation keeps the shape, size, and Mincho lettering of Standard Pieces, with different grain patterns and colors. Standard Pieces remains the default.
