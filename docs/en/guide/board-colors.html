Game Appearance

Combine five components using visual samples

Open the Appearance Window

Choose View → Game Appearance…. This replaces the former Piece Style and Board Colors menus.

Select samples on the left and check the combined result in the preview on the right. Your choices apply to all boards immediately and are saved automatically.

Choose Components Independently

TabSamplesChanges
Pieces31Standard, tiger grain, wood tones, light colors, dark colors, Sengoku Kanji, and chess-inspired pieces. Filter by family.
Board23Board color and grain, and grid lines.
Background23The background color around the board and stands.
Stands23Color and grain of the captured piece stands.
Players20Cards, turn badges, player names, clocks, and time warning colors.

Choosing one component preserves the others. Changing the board preserves the background; changing the background preserves the board color, grain, and grid lines. Board and stand grain can also be set independently.

Suggested Combinations and Preview

Fifteen combinations, including Ebony / Moon White, Pale Wood / Ink Night, and nine chess-inspired sets, provide starting points. A suggested combination changes all five components, including the background. You can then choose each component separately.

The preview uses the same renderer as the game screen. Check promoted and captured pieces or flip the preview to assess readability. These preview controls do not change a game in progress.

20 Player Information Styles

Choose light or dark cards, including Spring Green, Linen, Porcelain, Cherry Mist, Wisteria Mist, Celadon, Pale Aqua, Sand Dune, Amber, Walnut, Charcoal, Indigo Night, Forest Green, Grape, Iron Navy, Ink and Gold, Silver Gray, Red Earth, Rock Garden, and Moon White.

Details and Saved Preferences

Details retains all 18 individual color controls, transparency, piece size (90–112%), shadows, and separate board and stand grain settings. Individual changes are shown as Custom.

Restore Opening Appearance restores the pieces, colors, and textures from when this window was opened. Restore Default Colors resets only the 18 colors.

The appearance, window size, selected tabs, and piece filter are remembered. The same appearance is used in variation boards, tsume boards, and exported board images.
