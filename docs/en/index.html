Highlights

A quick tour of studying, customizing, and solving puzzles in ShogiBoardQ.

Study & Analysis

Candidate moves as arrows,
the game's flow as a graph

Register a USI engine to explore any position in Consideration mode or analyze a whole game at once. The evaluation graph and the results table take you straight to the moves where the game turned.

  • Candidate moves appear as numbered arrows; drops start from the piece stand
  • Game Analysis lists each move's score, whether it matched the best move, and the score change
  • See the engine's best move as an arrow during games; engine-vs-engine games show both sides' thinking
  • Organize your study with the branch tree, bookmarks, and comments

Consideration Mode → Game Analysis → Playing Games →

Appearance

40 piece styles,
and a board you design

Open View → Game Appearance… to choose pieces, board, background, piece stands, and player cards from samples, and check the whole screen in the live preview.

  • 40 piece styles: standard, tiger grain, wood, light, deep, Sengoku Kanji, chess-inspired, and alphabet
  • 26 boards, backgrounds, and piece stands each, 20 player-card styles, and 24 suggested combinations
  • Your choice also applies to the PV board, tsume shogi, and exported images

Piece Styles → Game Appearance →

Tsume Shogi

Solve 6,000 tsume shogi puzzles
on the board

The release packages include 1,000 puzzles each of mate in 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, and 13. Pick one from the list and checkmate the king while the built-in Hayanagi engine defends.

  • Browse puzzles as board cards and filter them by progress
  • Mate within the remaining moves to solve it; valid alternative solutions count too
  • Review your attempt history and replay the solution
  • Create puzzles with Mate Search and the Tsume Shogi Generator (GUI and CLI)

Tsume Shogi Play → Tsume Shogi Generator → Mate Search →

Features

The User Guide explains every feature, organized by task.

Games and analysis use a shogi engine that supports the USI protocol. The release packages include the Hayanagi engine. We thank the developers of shogi engines for their work.

Playing

  • Playing Games

    Play human vs. engine, engine vs. engine, or human vs. human. Legal destinations are highlighted, and an interrupted game resumes with its position and clocks.

  • Hayanagi Shogi Engine

    The bundled USI engine. Register it to play and analyze right away; it also defends in Tsume Shogi Play.

  • CSA Network Play

    Connect to CSA-protocol servers such as floodgate and play as a human or with an engine.

  • Entering King Declaration

    Count jishogi points and check declarations under the 24-point and 27-point rules.

Study & Analysis

  • Consideration Mode

    Let an engine study any position and compare several lines with arrows.

  • Game Analysis

    Analyze a whole game and follow each move's score on the evaluation graph.

  • Opening Books

    Load opening books to show, play, and edit book moves for the current position.

  • Position Collection Viewer

    Browse SFEN position collections on a board and import a position into the main window.

Records & Positions

  • Game Record Display

    View moves, times, bookmarks, comments, and variations. Records and coordinates can use Western notation.

  • Game Record Management

    Load and save KIF, KI2, CSA, JKF, USI, SFEN, and USEN, and copy or paste records via the clipboard.

  • Board Editing

    Arrange pieces freely with the piece box, then start a game or analysis from that position.

  • Image Export

    Save or copy the board and the evaluation graph as images.

Tsume Shogi

  • Tsume Shogi Play

    Choose a puzzle, solve it on the board, and review your history and the solution.

  • Mate Search

    Search for a mate with a USI engine and show the mating line.

  • Tsume Shogi Generator

    Generate puzzles that match your conditions in the GUI or CLI, check them for alternative mates, and save them.

Setup & Appearance

  • Piece Styles

    40 styles in all, including tiger grain, Sengoku Kanji, chess-inspired, and alphabet pieces.

  • Game Appearance

    Combine pieces, board, background, piece stands, and player cards freely from samples.

  • Dock Panels

    Arrange the record, thinking, graph, and other panels freely and save your layout.

  • Menu Panel

    Reach commands quickly from an icon menu panel, and register your favorites.

  • Piece Sound

    Turn the move sound on or off and adjust its volume, pitch, and tone.

  • Language Support

    Japanese, English, and Chinese (Simplified or Traditional) interfaces, with a choice of interface font.

  • Cross-Platform Support

    Built with Qt 6 for Linux, macOS, and Windows.

AI Integration

  • AI Client Integration (MCP)

    Ask Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI, Codex CLI, and other AI clients to convert records, analyze, generate puzzles, and operate the app.

Download

Get the latest release from GitHub. ShogiBoardQ is free to use.

  • Windows

    64-bit Windows (x64)

    1. Download the ZIP and extract all files
    2. Run ShogiBoardQ.exe
    Windows (ZIP)

  • macOS

    Mac with Apple silicon

    1. Extract the ZIP and open the DMG
    2. Drag ShogiBoardQ.app to Applications
    3. If the first launch is blocked, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security
    macOS (ZIP)

  • Linux

    x86_64 (AppImage)

    1. Extract the ZIP
    2. Make ShogiBoardQ-linux-x86_64.AppImage executable and run it
    Linux (ZIP)

Every package includes the Hayanagi engine and the tsume shogi collections (6,000 puzzles). See the release notes (in Japanese) for system requirements and changes.
